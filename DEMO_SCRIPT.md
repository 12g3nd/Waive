# Waive — 2–3 minute demo script

The arc: **dread → a clock → a routed remedy → the not-at-fault catch → the same
engine on a totally different injustice.** Everything below runs locally with
`npm run dev`; no cloud, no API key required (the samples run fully offline).

> One-line setup before recording: `npm run dev`, open `http://localhost:3000`.
> Optional: `ollama serve` with `llama3.2` pulled to show model-polished / Spanish
> text. Without it, the deterministic fallback still tells the whole story.

---

## Beat 0 — The thesis (10s)

> "People don't lose these cases on the merits. They lose by **silence** — an
> intimidating notice, a short deadline, a remedy they never find in time. Waive is
> one engine that reads the notice and routes them to the escape hatch before the
> clock runs out."

Land on the home screen: *"Drop a notice. Watch dread become a plan."* Point at the
line: **"Deadlines and remedies are computed by tested code that shows its work. The
model only translates — it never decides your legal outcome."**

---

## Beat 1 — Dread → a clock (35s)

Click the **"SSDI overpayment — not at fault"** sample (the card shows a real-looking
SSA overpayment letter with a red 50%-withholding warning).

- A brief **"Decoding…"** state, then the reveal.
- **The clock is the thesis.** A live countdown ring — here ~9 days — sitting in the
  amber zone, marked **Protected window**. "This is the date the 50% withholding
  starts unless they act. It turns red as it shrinks."
- Next to it, the scary letter is **decoded**: issuer, recipient, amount, reference.

---

## Beat 2 — The not-at-fault catch (the jaw-drop) (30s)

Point at the amber **highlighter banner**: *"The catch they're counting on you to
miss."*

> "She reported her return to work; SSA kept paying her anyway. SSA's **own rules**
> presume that's **not her fault** — which unlocks a **waiver that can cancel the
> debt entirely**."

Note the **Automatic** strength and the citation chip — **20 C.F.R. § 404.510(g)** —
a real, verified source. "Every legal claim links to its actual source. **7 of 7
verified** in the Sources panel."

---

## Beat 3 — The routed remedy + show your work (25s)

Scroll to **Your route**: the routed remedy is highlighted — **Form SSA-632
(waiver)** — with the other paths and a one-line "why not" for each.

- Expand **"Show your work — how each deadline was computed"**: read one derivation
  aloud — *"notice date + 30 calendar days, rolled to the next business day."* "A
  judge can ask how any number was computed; it's all here, and unit-tested."
- Mention the honest limit: "Waive prepares the SSA-632 with her figures but does
  **not** pretend to adjudicate hardship — that escalates to a legal-aid clinic."
- (Optional) Hit **Read aloud**, or flip **EN/ES** to show plain-language access.

---

## Beat 4 — Integrity (don't fight everything) (20s)

Go back, pick **"SSDI overpayment — at fault, can repay"** — *the same notice,
different facts.*

> "This person didn't report and can afford it. A tool optimized for engagement
> would still push a waiver. Waive **won't** — watch."

It routes to **repay / installment plan** with an explicit **Integrity check**: *"we
are not routing you to a waiver — it would likely be denied where the person is at
fault."* "Integrity over engagement is the whole point."

---

## Beat 5 — The cross-domain reveal (the proof) (30s)

Go back, pick **"Debt lawsuit — Ontario, time-barred."**

> "Different country. Different injustice. A debt buyer suing on an old debt. This is
> the **exact same engine and UI** — we just added a rule pack."

- The clock is now an urgent **Defence deadline (Form 9A)** — days, red as it shrinks.
- The highlighter catch: **"This debt may be too old to be legally enforceable"** —
  backed by **Ontario's Limitations Act, 2002**, with the deterministic 2-year math
  shown (last activity vs. claim date).
- It also flags **make the debt buyer prove it owns the debt**.

> "Adding this was a rule pack + citations + a sample — **zero changes to the
> engine**. That's the scalability story: every 'notice + deadline + hidden remedy'
> injustice is the same machine."

---

## Beat 6 — Close (10s)

> "Deterministic where it's high-stakes, a translator everywhere else, every claim
> cited, integrity over engagement, and it runs locally with no cloud. **Waive: stop
> losing by silence.**"

End on the footer: *"Information and document preparation, not legal advice — a
force-multiplier for legal-aid orgs and advocates."*

---

### If something goes sideways

- **No model running?** Fine — samples are precomputed; the banner reads "offline
  mode" and the deterministic text still tells the whole story.
- **Want Spanish / read-aloud?** Toggle **ES** and hit **Read aloud** in Beat 3.
- **Upload your own?** The dropzone takes an image/PDF; with a vision model it reads
  it live, otherwise it points you to the samples.
